# Chapter 1 — The Seal

Rain stitched pale lines down the night watch window. On the desk, the
lantern still burned. Beside it, someone had left a sealed letter.

On a shelf by the door, the watch book lay shut, its brass clasp fastened.
An empty hook above it swung once in a draught and settled. The room
smelled of lamp oil and the wet wool of a coat that had hung there all
winter.

The envelope sat in the warm circle on the oak. One corner had darkened where
a drop had fallen from somebody's sleeve. The rest was dry. In the red wax
was the crooked outline of a little fish, pressed so hard that its tail had
split the edge of the stamp. The seal itself was whole.

Down at the quay, a mooring ring knocked against the stone. The rain filled
the pause between knocks.
