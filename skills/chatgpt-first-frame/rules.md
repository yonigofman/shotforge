# Rules — chatgpt-first-frame

Each line is a constraint the prompt must carry. What follows `(case: …)` is
why the rule exists; it stays in this file and out of the prompt.

- The frame is exactly the project's aspect ratio — (case: S11 came back 4:3 when it was left out)
- Eye level is stated for every gaze, with the face it rests on — (case: S04 looked over the head, S07 at the ceiling)
- The wall behind each character is named, with what it is made of — (case: wood panelling appeared in S02 and S12a)
- The windows are counted and placed relative to each character — (case: the window behind Dima went missing in S08)
- Head size is given relative to the frame, not only distance — (case: Dima came back too large in S09)
- No other patrons and no staff unless the blockout has them — (case: faces appeared behind the bar in S01)
- No signage and no neon beyond what the bible lists — (case: a neon sign appeared in S01)
